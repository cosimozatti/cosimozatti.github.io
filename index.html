Profile

Economist completing a dual master's at the London School of Economics (MPA in Economic Policy) and Sciences Po (Master in Public Policy), with experience in development finance at the FAO Investment Centre, where I worked on the EIB-FAO Partnership and the EU Global Gateway. My work ranges from the design of technical assistance for a public development bank in Somalia to an audit of where development finance institutions invest in six African markets. It draws on applied research in health and labour economics and on a co-authored report published by the European Investment Bank.

I came to development finance through a gap I keep finding in the data: money committed on paper often does not reach the farmer or the manufacturing firm that needs it. My studies in Bologna convinced me that neither markets nor national governments can close such gaps alone, and that policy work needs economics and politics at the same table. That is why I want to work in impact finance, helping institutions deliver on their mandates so that capital reaches the firms and farmers it was meant for. Studying in three countries and spending eight years with CISV International, running intercultural programmes for young people, taught me to work across languages and cultures, and to listen before I propose.
