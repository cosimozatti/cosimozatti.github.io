About

I am an economist and public-policy researcher on a dual Master's at the London School of Economics and Sciences Po, after graduating 110/110 cum laude from the University of Bologna in Economics, Politics and Social Sciences.

Most policy questions cannot be settled by microeconomics or by politics alone, so I work across both. What draws me is the political economy of good jobs, industrial strategy and state capacity — and, increasingly, the gap between what public and multilateral finance commits on paper and what it changes on the ground.

In practice that means owning a question end to end: framing it, building the dataset, choosing the method that will actually survive scrutiny, and writing the version a decision-maker reads. Recent work has meant screening a development bank's loan book against climate and gender criteria, designing a discrete choice experiment on health rationing, and turning a 19,067-respondent survey into findings I defended in front of senior EIB economists.
